    Due Date Calculator

    Calculate your baby's due date based on your last menstrual period or conception date.

    Calculate Your Due Date

    About Due Date Calculation

    The due date is calculated as 280 days (40 weeks) from the first day of your last menstrual period. This method assumes a 28-day menstrual cycle.

    • Only 5% of babies are born on their exact due date
    • Most babies are born between 37-42 weeks
    • Your healthcare provider may adjust your due date after ultrasound

    How to Count Kicks

    • Count 10 kicks and note how long it takes
    • Most babies complete 10 kicks within 2 hours
    • Best time to count is when baby is usually active
    • Lie on your left side for better counting
    • Contact your provider if it takes longer than 2 hours

    When to Call Your Provider

    5-1-1 Rule: Call when contractions are 5 minutes apart, lasting 1 minute, for 1 hour.
    • Contractions getting stronger and closer together
    • Can't walk or talk during contractions
    • Water breaks or bleeding occurs
    • Baby movements decrease significantly

    Breastfeeding Guide

    Complete guide to successful breastfeeding for new mothers.

    Getting Started

    First Latch

    Initiate breastfeeding within the first hour after birth when baby is most alert.

    Positioning

    Find comfortable positions like cradle hold, football hold, or side-lying.

    Latch Signs

    Look for wide mouth, flanged lips, and rhythmic sucking and swallowing.

    Feeding Schedule

    Newborn
    8-12 times per day (every 2-3 hours)
    1-3 Months
    7-9 times per day (every 2-4 hours)
    3-6 Months
    6-8 times per day (every 3-4 hours)

    Common Concerns

    Sore Nipples

    Ensure proper latch and use lanolin cream. Contact lactation consultant if pain persists.

    Low Milk Supply

    Nurse frequently, stay hydrated, and ensure proper nutrition. Consider pumping between feeds.

    Engorgement

    Nurse frequently, apply warm compresses before feeding, and cold compresses after.
