💡 Pregnancy Test Tips

Get the most accurate results with these testing guidelines.

⏰

Test at the Right Time

Wait until at least 10-12 days past ovulation for more accurate results.

🌅

Test First Morning

Use first morning urine when hCG levels are most concentrated.

💧

Don't Drink Too Much

Avoid excessive fluids before testing to prevent dilution of hCG.

📋

Follow Instructions

Read and follow the test instructions carefully for best results.

⏱️

Wait for Results

Follow the exact timing specified in the instructions.

🔄

Retest if Needed

Wait a few days and retest if you get a negative but still suspect pregnancy.
