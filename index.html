    How this estimate works

    Conception usually happens close to ovulation. These estimates are useful planning tools, but they are not a medical diagnosis.

    • Due date method estimates conception about 266 days before birth.

    Common early signs

    Missed period

    One of the clearest signals, especially if your cycles are usually regular.

    Body changes

    Breast tenderness, bloating, and fatigue can happen early as hormones change.

    Best time to test

    Testing is usually most accurate after a missed period or about 14 days after ovulation.

    When to call a doctor

    Seek care for severe pain, heavy bleeding, dizziness, or symptoms that feel unusual.

    How this checker calculates results

    This checker uses a simple point system. More specific signs add more points, and irregular cycles reduce confidence slightly.
    • Days late add 1 point for 1-2 days, 2 points for 3-6 days, and 4 points for 7 or more days.
    • Nausea, breast tenderness, and fatigue add 2 points each because they are more commonly reported.
    • Frequent urination, bloating, light spotting, heightened smell, and mild cramping add 1 point each.
    • Often irregular cycles subtract 1 point because timing is less specific.
    • A period that is 7 or more days late can raise the final result to a possible sign even when the total score is still under 5.

    0-4 points

    Symptoms are not very specific yet. Keep tracking and wait for a clearer testing window.

    5-7 points

    Some common early signs are present. Testing may be reasonable soon, depending on timing.

    8+ points

    Pregnancy is possible based on timing and symptoms. A home test is more reasonable now.

    Getting Started

    First Latch

    Initiate breastfeeding within the first hour after birth when baby is most alert.

    Find comfortable positions like cradle hold, football hold, or side-lying.

    Common Concerns

    Sore Nipples

    Ensure proper latch and use lanolin cream. Contact lactation consultant if pain persists.

    Low Milk Supply

    Nurse frequently, stay hydrated, and ensure proper nutrition. Consider pumping between feeds.

    Engorgement

    Nurse frequently, apply warm compresses before feeding, and cold compresses after.
