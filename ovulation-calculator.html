💡 Fertility Tips & Insights

Evidence-based tips to improve your conception chances.

🥗 Nutrition for Fertility

  • Eat foods rich in folate (leafy greens, beans)
  • Include healthy fats (avocado, nuts, olive oil)
  • Choose lean proteins (fish, chicken, legumes)
  • Limit processed foods and added sugars

🏃‍♀️ Lifestyle Factors

  • Maintain healthy weight (BMI 18.5-24.9)
  • Exercise moderately (30 mins daily)
  • Limit caffeine to 200mg/day
  • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol

💤 Sleep & Stress Management

  • Get 7-9 hours of quality sleep
  • Practice stress reduction (yoga, meditation)
  • Maintain consistent sleep schedule
  • Consider relaxation techniques

💊 Supplements & Health

  • Take prenatal vitamins with folic acid
  • Consider vitamin D and omega-3
  • Stay hydrated (8+ glasses water daily)
  • Regular check-ups with healthcare provider

🎯 Advanced Conception Tips

Maximize your chances with these evidence-based strategies.

⏰

Timing is Everything

Have intercourse every 1-2 days during your fertile window. Sperm can live 5 days, while the egg survives 24 hours.

🌡️

Track Your Temperature

Take your basal body temperature every morning before getting out of bed. A slight increase indicates ovulation has occurred.

💧

Monitor Cervical Mucus

Egg white cervical mucus indicates peak fertility. It's stretchy, clear, and helps sperm travel to the egg.

🧘‍♀️

Reduce Stress

High stress levels can affect hormone production and ovulation. Try yoga, meditation, or deep breathing exercises.

🥦

Eat Fertility-Boosting Foods

Focus on whole foods, antioxidants, and healthy fats. Avoid trans fats and limit processed foods.

💪

Maintain Healthy Weight

Both underweight and overweight can affect fertility. Aim for a healthy BMI through balanced diet and exercise.
